Privacy Policy for Masar AI

Effective Date: November 2026

Masar AI ("we", "our", or "us") provides automated customer service and sales assistant solutions for businesses operating on Meta platforms (Instagram, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information processed through our application.

1. Information We Collect

When a user interacts with a business page powered by Masar AI, we may process:

  • Messaging Data: Text messages, inquiries, and order details sent by customers to the connected business account.
  • Profile Identifiers: Platform-scoped User IDs (PSID / IGSID), display names, or phone numbers necessary to reply to the conversation and fulfill customer orders.
  • Business Account Tokens: OAuth access tokens authorized explicitly by business owners to manage their automated responses.

2. How We Use Information

We strictly use the collected data to:

  • Generate accurate, automated responses to customer inquiries regarding products, pricing, and delivery.
  • Record confirmed customer orders requested by the user during the chat.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the automated messaging service for the connected business.

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data or conversation logs to third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access tokens and conversation records are stored on secured servers with strict access controls and are retained only as long as necessary to provide the service.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform rules, users and business owners have the full right to request the deletion of their data stored by Masar AI at any time:

  1. Revoke App Access: Business owners can immediately disconnect Masar AI by going to their Facebook/Instagram Settings > Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations) and removing Masar AI.
  2. Request Complete Data Erasure: To permanently delete all chat logs, user identifiers, or order records associated with your account, send an email to support@masarx.me with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your username or phone number.

All associated records will be permanently purged from our systems within 48 hours of receiving your request.
