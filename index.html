Privacy Policy for Masar AI

Effective Date: November 2026

Masar AI ("we", "our", or "us") provides automated customer service, sales assistant, and CRM solutions for businesses operating on Meta platforms (Instagram, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information processed through our application.

1. Information We Collect

When a user interacts with a business account powered by Masar AI, we may collect and process:

  • Messaging & Order Data: Text messages, product inquiries, phone numbers, physical delivery addresses (including city, neighborhood, and landmarks), and order details voluntarily provided by customers during chat interactions.
  • Profile Identifiers: Platform-scoped User IDs (PSID / IGSID) and display names necessary to maintain conversation context and fulfill orders.
  • Business Account Tokens: OAuth access tokens authorized explicitly by business owners to manage their automated responses and CRM records.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Automated Customer Support & Fulfillment: Generating accurate responses regarding products, pricing, and delivery, and recording confirmed customer orders.
  • Business CRM Management: Organizing customer contact details, delivery addresses, and order history within isolated CRM databases accessible exclusively to the respective business owner.
  • Service Improvement & Analytics: Using strictly anonymized and aggregated interaction data (stripped of all personal identifiers, phone numbers, and addresses) to improve our AI language models, system accuracy, and internal analytics.

We never sell, rent, or trade personally identifiable user data or customer lists to third parties or external advertisers.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access tokens, CRM records, and conversation logs are stored on secured servers with strict tenant isolation and access controls, and are retained only as long as necessary to provide the service.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform rules, users and business owners have the full right to request the deletion of their data stored by Masar AI at any time:

  1. Revoke App Access: Business owners can immediately disconnect Masar AI by going to their Facebook/Instagram Settings > Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations) and removing Masar AI.
  2. Request Complete Data Erasure: To permanently delete all chat logs, delivery addresses, CRM records, or user identifiers associated with your account, send an email to support@masarx.me with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your username or phone number.

All associated records will be permanently purged from our systems within 48 hours of receiving your request.
